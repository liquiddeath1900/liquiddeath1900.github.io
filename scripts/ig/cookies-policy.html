1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for the site to work. Others help us understand how people use it.

3. Third-party cookies

Google sets its own cookies under its own privacy policy. We don't control them. You can read Google's privacy policy.

4. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work.

To opt out of Google Analytics on every site, you can use Google's opt-out add-on.

5. Changes
